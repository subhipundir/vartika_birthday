Dear Vartika, Happy Birthday, my bestie! 🎉 We met in the first year of college, and I had no idea back then that this random friendship would become my favourite part of these three years. Remember how I was all alone at the start of college? You were the one who talked to me first, and I will never forget that. 🥹 Since then you have been my coffee buddy ☕, my gossip partner (spilling the tea on everyone is our favourite sport 🍵😜), and my partner in crime on mornings when we skip breakfast and end up eating macaroni 🍝. And how can I forget: whenever you don't want to attend a class, it's "You're the CR, get the class cancelled!" 😂 And if I don't listen, guess who gets scolded? Me, of course 😅 But you are also the one who takes care of me every single time. That scolding is just love in disguise 💕 Also, thank you for praising my cooking every single time, even on the days it did not turn out that good 🙈 You are the sweetest food critic ever 🍽️ One memory I can never forget: when someone who was once your best friend had a fight with me, you left her side and came to support me. That meant the world to me, and I will always remember it. I just wish that you stay happy like this always, keep smiling, and that all your dreams come true. Love you loads, always on your side 💕 – Subhi
